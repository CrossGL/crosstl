CrossTL translated each native SPIR-V probe whose feature Rust has too straight to Rust, and rust-gpu checked the result. Its compute probes also ran on a runtime. [COVERAGE.md](../COVERAGE.md#translation-matrix) describes the matrix and has every other cell.
