# CrossTL translation matrix: Slang to Rust

Measured on commit [`8f9a2e8`](https://github.com/CrossGL/crosstl/commit/8f9a2e88b1fda43915bc783f1c4a52557476f9a9) at 2026-11-28T09:51:00Z by the [Coverage workflow](https://github.com/CrossGL/crosstl/actions/workflows/coverage.yml). CrossTL translated each native Slang probe whose feature Rust has too straight to Rust, and rust-gpu checked the result. Its compute probes also ran on a runtime. [COVERAGE.md](../COVERAGE.md#translation-matrix) describes the matrix and has every other cell.
