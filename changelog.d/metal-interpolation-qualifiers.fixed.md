Metal output keeps the interpolation of a fragment entry parameter (`nointerpolation float2 uv : TEXCOORD0` now has `[[flat]]`) and combines a mode with a sampling location into one attribute (`noperspective centroid` is `[[centroid_no_perspective]]`, no longer `[[center_no_perspective]]`). Metal input imports `[[centroid_perspective]]`, `[[sample_no_perspective]]` and the other combined attributes as the mode and sampling every generator reads, and `[[amplification_id]]` as the view index.
