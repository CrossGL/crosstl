A CrossGL declaration whose initializer is a vector or matrix of another shape, such as `vec4 m = a;` with `vec3 a` or `mat3x4 m = a;` with `mat4x3 a`, is refused with `InitializerShapeMismatchError` (`project.translate.initializer-shape-mismatch`), which names the variable, its type and the initializer's type. Vulkan output used to store the value through a pointer of the declared type, which `spirv-val` rejects, and the other generators copied the declaration to compilers that reject it. The DXR transform calls have their CrossGL types, so `mat3x4 m = ObjectToWorld3x4();` is refused too. A scalar initializer is left alone (#4599).
