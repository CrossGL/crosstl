HLSL output declares a ray tracing shader record block (GLSL `shaderRecordEXT`, DXC `[[vk::shader_record_ext]]`) instead of refusing it: SPIR-V reads it from the `ShaderRecordBufferKHR` storage class, and Direct3D 12 reads it as root constants of a `LocalRootSignature` subobject that the program associates with each entry point that reads the record. A record whose bytes DXC or HLSL constant-buffer packing would place differently from its std430, std140 or scalar layout, a matrix member, a record a non-ray stage reads, or one over 4064 bytes is refused with `HlslShaderRecordError`.
