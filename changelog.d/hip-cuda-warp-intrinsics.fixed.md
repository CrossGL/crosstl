HIP output computes the CUDA warp intrinsics a CrossGL program calls (`__shfl_sync`, `__shfl_up_sync`, `__shfl_down_sync`, `__shfl_xor_sync`, `__ballot_sync`, `__any_sync`, `__all_sync`, `__uni_sync`, `__activemask`, `__match_any_sync`, `__match_all_sync`, the `__reduce_*_sync` family and `__syncwarp`) over the caller's 32-lane segment of the wavefront, with HIP's `__shfl` family and `__ballot`, so they compute what CUDA computes on 32- and 64-lane wavefronts. HIP output used to print them verbatim, which hipcc 5.7 rejects, and wrote `__shfl_down_sync` as a helper that returned its own operand unless `HIP_ENABLE_WARP_SYNC_BUILTINS` was defined.
