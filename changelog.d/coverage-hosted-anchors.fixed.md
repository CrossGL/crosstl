Coverage: translation matrix cells no longer report a translation as wrong for an anchor its source probe cannot carry. A translation imported from Metal, CUDA, HIP or HLSL is not checked for a storage-image format, which those languages take from the texture, surface or view the host binds, and one imported from CUDA or HIP is not checked for the flattened thread index, lane index, warp index or warp count that their probes compute from the built-in variables they have.
