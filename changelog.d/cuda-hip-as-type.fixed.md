CUDA and HIP output write CrossGL's `as_type<T>(value)`, such as one imported from Metal, with the runtimes' bit-cast intrinsics (`__float_as_uint`, `__double_as_longlong`, `__half_as_ushort` and their inverses). They used to copy the call into the output, where nvcc and hipcc rejected the undeclared identifier. A reinterpretation between types of different sizes is refused with a diagnostic.
