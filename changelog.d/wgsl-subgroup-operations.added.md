WGSL output writes CrossGL wave operations as WGSL's subgroup and quad built-in functions: `WaveActiveSum` and the other reductions as `subgroupAdd`, `subgroupMul`, `subgroupMin`, `subgroupMax`, `subgroupAnd`, `subgroupOr` and `subgroupXor`, the sum and product scans as `subgroupExclusiveAdd` and its inclusive and `Mul` forms, votes and ballots as `subgroupAll`, `subgroupAny` and `subgroupBallot`, lane reads as `subgroupBroadcastFirst`, `subgroupBroadcast`, `subgroupShuffle` and its `Xor`, `Up` and `Down` forms, and quad reads as `quadSwapX`, `quadSwapY`, `quadSwapDiagonal` and `quadBroadcast`. `WaveGetLaneIndex()`, `WaveGetLaneCount()` and `WaveIsFirstLane()` read the entry point's `subgroup_invocation_id` and `subgroup_size` inputs, and `bool` operands of the lane reads and bitwise reductions travel as `u32`. Every wave operation used to be refused; those WGSL has no built-in for are still refused by name.
