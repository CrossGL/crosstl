Slang output refuses derivative operations (`dFdx`, `dFdy`, `fwidth` and the coarse/fine forms, and biased samples that lower to `SampleBias`) in a compute stage whose thread group is not a derivative group, with `SlangLoweringRefused` naming the operation and the thread group. A group with y == 1 needs x to be a multiple of four (linear quads); a group with y > 1 needs even x and y (2x2 quads). It used to write the operations, which slangc rejects with error E31210 or E31211 (#4657).
