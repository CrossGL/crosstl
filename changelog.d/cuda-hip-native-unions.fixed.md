CUDA and HIP output write a CrossGL `@union_layout` struct, such as one imported from a CUDA or Metal union, as a C++ `union` with a `static_assert` of its recorded size and alignment. They used to write a `struct` whose members had separate storage, so a kernel that wrote one member and read another read a value that was never stored (#4774). A union whose recorded member offsets, sizes or alignments C++ cannot reproduce is refused with a diagnostic.
