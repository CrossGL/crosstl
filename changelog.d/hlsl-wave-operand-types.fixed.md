HLSL output generates the operands of a wave intrinsic in their own type: the lane index of `WaveReadLaneAt` and `QuadReadLaneAt` as `uint`, the partition mask of a `WaveMultiPrefix` operation as `uint4`, and the operands of ballots, bit counts, votes and matches in the type they have. A `WaveGetLaneIndex()` inside the lane index of a `float4` `WaveReadLaneAt` used to be checked against `float4` and refused.
