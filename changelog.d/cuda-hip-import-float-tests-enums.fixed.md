CUDA and HIP input now import `__isinff`, `__isnanf`, `__finitef`, `__signbitf` and their double forms as integer results of CrossGL's `isinf`, `isnan`, `isfinite` and `signbit`; name unscoped enumerators through their enum (`Mode::Low`); keep `volatile T*` and `T* __restrict__` buffer parameters as `@volatile` and `@restrict`; read HIP's `__lane_id()` as the subgroup invocation index; and write `acoshf`, `asinhf`, `atanhf`, `copysignf`, `cospif`, `sinpif`, `exp10f`, `fdimf`, `ilogbf`, `ldexpf`, `log10f` and `nextafterf` under CrossGL's unsuffixed names. Each of these reached Metal, GLSL, HLSL or SPIR-V as an undeclared name or lost its qualifier. CUDA and HIP output keep `exp10` as `exp10f` or `exp10` instead of `exp2(x * log2(10))`, and CUDA output writes a `@volatile` storage buffer as a pointer to `volatile` memory, as HIP output does.
