Metal input inlines a Boolean dispatch callback once when its body calls `threadgroup_barrier`, `simdgroup_barrier` or a `simd_` or `quad_` function and it reads its tag only in `if`, `if constexpr` and `while` conditions: the tag becomes a `bool` that the call's condition initializes. The barrier then stays in the caller's control flow. The importer used to emit one instantiation per tag value under the dispatch condition, so a condition that differs between the SIMD-groups of a threadgroup put the barrier under non-uniform control flow, which DirectX and OpenGL output refused. Other callbacks keep their two instantiations.
