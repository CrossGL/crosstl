OpenCL C input also reads numbered and half vector component selections (`v.s01`, `v.S3`, `v.lo`, `v.hi`, `v.even`, `v.odd`, and selections of selections such as `v.odd.hi`) as the CrossGL `xyzw` swizzles they select, as values and as assignment targets, instead of copying the OpenCL suffix to a target that rejects it. A selection CrossGL cannot write is refused with `OpenCLVectorComponentUnsupportedError`: one that reads past the vector's components, several components of an 8- or 16-component vector, a selection that includes the undefined `w` of a 3-component vector's `.hi` or `.odd`, or an assignment target that repeats a component. `cross` of two `float4` or `double4` vectors returns the cross product of their `xyz` components with a zero `w`, as OpenCL C defines it, instead of a 3-component value. An array extent made of integer literals and operators (`float s[4 * 2]`, or a product of object-like macros) folds to its value with C integer arithmetic; an extent that names a constant stays an expression; a non-positive extent, or one that is not an integer constant expression, is refused with `OpenCLArrayExtentUnsupportedError`.
