CUDA and HIP input pass the outputs of `sincos`, `sincosf`, `__sincosf`, `sincospi`, `sincospif`, `frexp`, `frexpf`, `modf`, `modff`, `remquo` and `remquof` as CrossGL `out` arguments instead of copying `&s` into the call, which the CUDA and HIP generators refused. A `sincosf` or `sincos` statement whose operand has no side effects becomes two assignments that keep the CUDA accuracy bound on every target.
