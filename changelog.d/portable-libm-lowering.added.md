CrossGL `sinpi`, `cospi`, `fdim`, `nextafter`, `ilogb` and `copysign` translate to every target. GLSL, HLSL, SPIR-V, WGSL, Slang, Rust and Mojo output call CrossGL helpers built from bit operations and correctly rounded binary32 arithmetic (`sinpi`/`cospi` within 1 ulp over every binary32 operand); CUDA and HIP output compute vector `fdim`, `nextafter`, `ilogb` and `copysign` per component; Metal output gives the zeros of `sinpi` and `cospi` their C sign.
