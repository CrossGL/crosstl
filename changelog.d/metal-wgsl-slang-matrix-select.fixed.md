Metal, WGSL and Slang output now choose between matrices with a conditional instead of `select(...)`, which their compilers reject: `select(m1, m0, c)` becomes `c ? m0 : m1` on Metal and Slang, and an `if`/`else` on the whole initializer or assignment on WGSL (a matrix `select` anywhere else is refused, as a matrix ternary already is). HLSL and SPIR-V output are unchanged (#4525).
