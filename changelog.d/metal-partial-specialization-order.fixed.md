Metal input selects the most specialized of several matching struct partial specializations, as in `Cell<float, uint>` with `Cell<T, U, enable_if_t<true>>` and `Cell<T, uint, void>`, or `Cell<float, device uint*>` with `Cell<T, device U*>` and `Cell<T, device uint*>`, and completes a partial specialization's arguments with the primary template's defaults. It used to refuse the first, give the second the primary template's fields without a diagnostic, and ignore a partial specialization that omitted a defaulted argument. A template-id that no candidate orders is refused as ambiguous, and one whose ordering depends on an enable_if of the candidate's own parameters is refused as unsupported (#1987).
