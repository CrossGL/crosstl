GLSL output writes HLSL `f32tof16` and `f16tof32` with `packHalf2x16` and `unpackHalf2x16`, componentwise for vectors, instead of refusing them as undeclared functions. HLSL `InterlockedCompareExchangeFloatBitwise` and `InterlockedCompareStoreFloatBitwise` import as CrossGL `atomicCompSwap` on the float word, which GLSL and GLSL ES output compare bitwise; targets without float compare-and-swap still refuse them.
