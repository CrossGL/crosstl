CUDA and HIP input write `__clz`, `__clzll`, `__byte_perm`, `__funnelshift_l`, `__funnelshift_lc`, `__funnelshift_r` and `__funnelshift_rc` with CrossGL bit operations that every target declares, and `fdivide` and `fdividef` as a division. CUDA input refused `__byte_perm` with any selector but `0x0123` and every funnel shift, both importers wrote `__clz` as `countLeadingZeros`, which CrossGL does not declare, and HIP input read selector nibbles 8 to 15 as zero bytes where CUDA and HIP read byte `nibble & 7`.
