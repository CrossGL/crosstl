A CrossGL variable in the `device` address space, such as `device float acc[64];` or `var<device> acc: array<f32, 64>;` at shader or stage scope, is refused with a `DeviceVariableUnsupportedError` that names the variable and the target for every target except CUDA, HIP and CrossGL. HLSL, GLSL, WebGL and Slang output used to declare it as a read-only uniform or drop it, Metal output declared a program-scope `device` variable that the Metal compiler rejects, and WGSL, SPIR-V, Rust and Mojo output gave every invocation its own copy (#4726).
